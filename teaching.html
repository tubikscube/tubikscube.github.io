02 / Projects

University of Calgary Grant recipient & lead researcher

Charred Chronicles: A Transdisciplinary Inquiry

Awarded a Transdisciplinary Scholarship Connector Grant to lead a trauma-informed study of person–place relationships, non-economic loss and community recovery after the Lytton Creek Fire.

Project details

Climate change is escalating extreme events globally, with North America’s intensifying wildfires as a prime example. Beyond the immediate economic and physical toll, these events deeply affect communities’ sense of home, identity and safety — triggering significant emotional and non-economic losses and damages (NELD).

The project connects academic disciplines, institutions, Indigenous and non-Indigenous stakeholders and disaster-response organizations, adopting a trauma-informed, reflexive and collective approach — constructing knowledge with society, not for it — to support culturally relevant climate response.

DESTECTIC Lab Lead graduate researcher

Automated Deployable Camp Layout Technology

Research into temporary shelter design and AI-supported camp layouts for safer, more efficient responses to forced displacement.

Project details

With Dr. Jinmo Rhee. The study proposes AI-driven design automation to expedite well-organized camp layouts, so that immediate and provisional settlements foster safety and integration.

My role includes an in-depth literature review on temporary shelter design, the mental and physical impacts of shelters, and their real-world applications for forcibly displaced populations — analysing current practice alongside guidelines from UNHCR, the Red Cross and other agencies.

Rutgers / UCL Curator & lead researcher

The Anti-Eugenics Project: Legacies / Reckonings / Futures

An interdisciplinary network examining the continuing legacies and harms of eugenicist ideologies.

Visit project ↗

Project details

Founded by Dr. Jack Tchen (Rutgers) and Benedict Ipgrave (UCL), the network brings together scholars, organizers, cultural workers and artists to excavate the lasting effects of eugenicist thought and begin dismantling the systems of exclusion that persist as racism, classism, misogyny, ableism, homophobia, transphobia and xenophobia.

My contribution covered archival research, digital production and curation for a ten-day convening and a public online resource.
